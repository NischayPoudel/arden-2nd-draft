Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Arden The Beauty Point & Academy respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and appointments, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

When you request an appointment or contact us, we may receive your name, phone number, email address, preferred service, preferred expert, requested date and time, and any message or special requirements you provide. We may also receive information you voluntarily share during a consultation or academy enquiry.

How We Use Information

We use information to respond to enquiries, arrange and confirm appointments, provide requested salon or academy services, communicate changes, maintain service records where appropriate, improve our customer experience, and meet applicable legal or operational requirements.

WhatsApp and External Services

Website forms open a prefilled WhatsApp conversation with Arden. Information submitted through WhatsApp is also handled under WhatsApp’s own privacy terms. Our website may link to Google Maps and other third-party services; their privacy practices are controlled by those providers.

Sharing and Protection

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with staff or service providers only where reasonably necessary to fulfil your request, operate the website, or comply with law. We take reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Retention and Your Choices

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including appointment administration, customer service, recordkeeping, and legal obligations. You may ask us to correct or delete information, subject to lawful retention requirements.

Photography and Media

We will seek appropriate permission before using identifiable client photographs or videos for promotional purposes. You may withdraw future-use permission by contacting us, although this may not affect material already lawfully published or printed.
