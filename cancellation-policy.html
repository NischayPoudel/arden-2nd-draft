Arden Policies

Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

We understand that plans can change. Please contact Arden as early as possible when you need to cancel, reschedule, or change an appointment so we can assist you and offer the time to another client.

Changing an Appointment

Requests to cancel, reschedule, change services, or change the number of clients are subject to availability and are not effective until acknowledged by Arden. Contact us through WhatsApp or phone using the details below.

Late Cancellations and Missed Appointments

Where a cancellation is made close to the appointment time, or a client does not attend, Arden may be unable to refund or transfer an advance payment because staff time, products, preparation, or travel may already have been reserved. Any applicable charge or deposit treatment will be based on the terms communicated and accepted when the booking was confirmed.

Late Arrival

If you arrive late, we will try to provide the service without disrupting later appointments. Depending on the remaining time, the service may need to be shortened, changed, or rescheduled. The confirmed charge may still apply where time and resources were reserved.

Bridal, Group and Off-Site Bookings

Bridal, couple, group, event, and off-site appointments often require advance planning and reserved staff. These bookings may have specific cancellation dates, non-refundable preparation or travel costs, and deposit terms. The written terms confirmed for your booking will apply.

Academy Courses

Course cancellations, transfers, missed classes, materials, and refunds are governed by the terms communicated for the selected programme. Where Arden permits a transfer, it remains subject to seat and schedule availability.

Cancellations by Arden

If Arden must cancel or materially change a confirmed booking, we will contact you as soon as reasonably possible and offer an appropriate reschedule or address any payment received for the affected service. Arden is not responsible for unrelated travel, accommodation, or third-party costs unless required by law.

How to Contact Us

To cancel or reschedule, call +977 985-1211778 or message us on WhatsApp. Please include the booking name, service, date, and time.
